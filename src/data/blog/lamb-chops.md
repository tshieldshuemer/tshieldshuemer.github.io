### Prep

First I patted the chops dry with a paper towel, then seasoned them with a decent amount of salt & pepper and a few chilli flakes.

<img src="/Raw.jpg" alt="Raw Seasoned Chops" width="600" />

### Cooking
I brought the pan to a high temperature, then added almond oil, followed by the chops, which were seared for 2 minutes on each side.

I aslo threw in a knob of butter for the last 30 seconds.

### Sides
In accompaniment, I prepared some plain white rice - seasoned with paprika and caraway seeds, and fried up some onions - seasoned with smoked paprika and glazed with teryaki sauce. 

<img src="/Onions.jpeg" alt="Onions" width="600" />

### Review

<img src="/PlateUp.jpeg" alt="Onions" width="600" />

Overall it wasn't a bad meal: The chops were cooked just right and the onions turned out quite nicely. The rice left some room for improvement - it was slightly too sticky. 
Other than that, some garlic and rosemary could have paired really well with the lamb but I didn't have access to any.
