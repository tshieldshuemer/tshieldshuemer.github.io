### Dart Board Ring Light

Throwing darts in the dark had mixed results - so I put together a simple ring light which hooks directly onto the board

<img src="/Main Render.png" alt="Main Dart Board Render" width="1200" class="lightbox-trigger border-4 border-black cursor-pointer hover:opacity-90 transition"/>

The piece consists of two 3D printed halfs which are slot joined at the middle. The left half houses a simple battery box to power a 5V LED strip which spans the surface of the inner arc.
