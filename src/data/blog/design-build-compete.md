## Background:
This college project tasked me with designing and manufacturing a simple battery powered car to carry a water bottle up a ramp as fast as possible. Design choices related to selection of wheel and pulley types & ratios/configurations as well as full freedom in terms of geometry design, limited only by material. The car was manufactured using simple sheet metal manufacturing processes such as shearing, bending, riveting and sawing.
