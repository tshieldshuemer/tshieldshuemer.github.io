## Gear:
Scuba diving is a hugely expensive hobby; On this blog I've compiled all of my initial expenses to get my own kit and a CMAS 1* dive Qualification.

My first big investment was getting a decent semi-drysuit: Irish retailers were quite expensive at the time, so I ended up importing from the UK. I had to pay nearly €100 in import taxes but it still worked out cheaper.

- Semi-drysuit: Northern Diver Delta Flex Medium - €230
- Fins: Northern Diver Rubber Jetfins XXL Metal Buckle - €52
- Import Taxes & Fees: €96

 ## Membership & Service Costs:
 - Diving Ireland Student Diver Membership: €65
 - Club Membership: €60
 - Diver 1* Course Fee: €60

### Total Membership & Service Costs: €185

 # Overall: €907
When it comes to kit, you can definitely do it for a lot cheaper: I'd recommend trying to find a second hand semidry. I also splashed a bit of cash when it came to the fins, compass and dive knife. For the purpose of a base qualification a compass isn't required either.
