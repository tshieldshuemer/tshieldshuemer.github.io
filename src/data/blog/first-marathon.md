## Strategy
The furthest I had run previously was 30k.
A big part of me being able to do this was how I approached it mentally;
I found that by going into it through a lense of accepting that this is what you will be doing for the next 4-5 hours made it quite manageable. Instead of counting down the kilometers to the finish line I found I was able to just zone out comfortably into the process for the majority of the run.

In terms of preparing food/carb loading for this run I regrettably did nothing, but I made sure to make stops along the way:
- 10k: **powerade** & a bag of **natural confectionary dinosaurs**
- 15k: **powerade**
- 24k: **chocolate bar** and a pint of **water**
- Last 10k: two more bottles of **water**

- Garmin *(In Navy)* shows the full run time including breaks
- Strava *(In Orange)* shows moving time only 
- Both datasets have a 3rd Order Polynomial trend line fitted

## Challenges
At 37km my right leg started suffering from a multitude of cramps. I stopped briefly to try and iron them out and managed to get them down to a tolerable level - with only 5k to go it was manageable to suffer through a bit 

## Aftermath
I was unsurprisingly quite shattered afterwards, having **burnt circa 3,000kcal** *(12.56 MJ, or roughly the energy in 0.3 kg of jet fuel burned)*

In compensation I had 1kg of chicken thighs and a load of rice - then hit a deep sleep in my Hammock.
