## Centauri Carbon

<img src="/CentauriCarbon.png" alt="CentauriCarbon" width="1200" />

I bought my printer in June of 2025 for € 358,99 - it's served me well since without issue 
### Features:
- Fully-enclosed CoreXY frame
- 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume
  
### Printing Capabilities:
- Supports PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU, PET, PC, PA (Nylon), and carbon/glass fiber reinforced variants
- Up to 500 mm/s with 20,000 mm/s² acceleration, and a 32 mm³/s flow rate

  **So far I have only printed in PLA, PETG and TPU**
